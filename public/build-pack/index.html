Get the material right

Exactly 2 A4 sheets total

  • 1 existing A4 base — 210 × 297 mm. Use your existing 3 mm cardboard.
  • 1 extra A4 of 3 mm card — all 13 walls in six rows.
  • No roof. Reuse wall cutouts for moving door/window leaves.

The nesting layout keeps 10 mm outer margins, 2 mm horizontal cut gaps and 5 mm between rows. No other cardboard thickness is required. Use tape hinges; optional furniture can use scraps.

Assembly order

  1. Verify the 50 mm bar, then glue the floor plan onto the existing A4 base without stretching.
  2. Measure actual stock thickness. Cut and label W01–W13 using the single wall-stock nesting sheet; remove the door/window holes before assembly.
  3. W01 and W02 span the full width. W03 and W04 butt between them. Confirm the outer rectangle is 187.69 × 118.46 mm before gluing.
  4. Fit W05 between the exterior walls. W06–W08 butt between W01 and W05. W09 fits between W06 and W07. W10 fits between W05 and W02, W11 between W03 and W10, W12 starts at W10’s east face, and W13 butts below W12 and against W02.
  5. D02 master-bedroom clear width is 11.577 mm after trimming its east edge to the partition face. Bedroom 2, bedroom 3 and kitchen junctions are effectively flush (0.038 mm theoretical clearance); reinforce them with tape.
  6. Mount tape hinges on the inner wall face so reused 3 mm leaves sit proud of the wall when closed. Use lift-out window inserts with tape pull-tabs; optional sliding rails need a measured hardware fit. Outward-swinging windows exceed the A4 side margin. Check door swing and servo travel before adding furniture.
  7. Build the removable electronics panel after measuring actual parts. Route wires away from moving leaves. Keep the model roofless for visibility and repair.
  8. Label door/window controls with the matching D/G identifiers. Test one physical input at a time against the app. Add optional furniture only after the controls work.
